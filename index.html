🥩 Main Ingredients

🧂 Seasoning

Beef: 1 tsp salt · 1 tsp black pepper · 1 tsp garlic powder · 1 tsp onion powder · Potatoes: 1 tsp salt · ½ tsp pepper · 1 tsp garlic powder · 1 tsp paprika

Sauce (Big Mac-style): 150g 0% Greek yogurt · 1 tbsp light mayo · 1 tbsp ketchup · 1 tsp yellow mustard · 1 tbsp finely chopped dill pickles · ½ tsp garlic powder · ½ tsp onion powder · ½ tsp paprika · salt & pepper to taste

👨‍🍳 Instructions

  1. Potatoes: Peel & cube 300g potatoes into ~2cm chunks. Boil in salted water 7–8 min (fork-tender). Drain and SHAKE vigorously in colander to rough up edges. Toss with 1 tbsp olive oil, 1 tsp salt, ½ tsp pepper, 1 tsp garlic powder, 1 tsp paprika. Spread on baking sheet — don't crowd. Roast at 200°C fan (ventilacija/konvekcija) for 25–30 min, flipping halfway, until golden and crispy.
  2. Beef: Heat a skillet over high. Crumble 500g lean ground beef into pan. Sear without stirring 2 min for color. Break up, season with salt, pepper, garlic powder, onion powder. Cook 6–8 min until browned with crispy edges. Drain excess fat if needed (especially with 85/15).
  3. Sauce: In a small bowl, combine 150g 0% Greek yogurt + 1 tbsp light mayo + 1 tbsp ketchup + 1 tsp yellow mustard + 1 tbsp finely chopped dill pickles + ½ tsp garlic powder + ½ tsp onion powder + ½ tsp paprika. Stir. Season with salt & pepper. Add 1 tsp pickle juice for extra tang if desired.
  4. Assemble: Bowl → crispy potatoes → seasoned beef → diced onion (60g) → drizzle Big Mac sauce. Optional: shredded lettuce, chopped pickles on top.

🔥 Base Method (all bowls follow this)

  1. Potatoes: Boil 7–8 min → drain & SHAKE → toss with oil + seasoning per recipe → roast at 200°C fan 25–30 min
  2. Beef: Sear 500g ground beef in hot pan — break up — season per recipe — cook through
  3. Sauce: All use 150g 0% Greek yogurt base + ingredients per recipe
