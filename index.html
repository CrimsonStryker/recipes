🍔 Boy Kibble Big Mac Bowl

High-protein crispy potato & beef bowl with Big Mac-style sauce

🥩 Main Ingredients

🧂 Seasoning & Sauce

Salt, pepper, garlic powder, onion powder · Big Mac sauce: yogurt + light mayo + ketchup + mustard + pickles + spices

👨‍🍳 Instructions

  1. Cut potatoes into 1cm cubes. Toss with olive oil, salt, paprika. Bake at 200°C for 30-35 min, flipping halfway, until golden and crispy.
  2. Sear beef in a hot pan, break up, season. Cook 6-8 min until browned.
  3. Mix sauce ingredients together. Adjust to taste.
  4. Assemble: potatoes → beef → veg → sauce.

🔥 Base Method (all bowls)

  1. Potatoes: Boil 7-8 min → drain & SHAKE in colander → toss with oil & seasoning → roast at 200°C fan 25-30 min
  2. Beef: Sear 500g lean ground beef in hot pan, break up, season per bowl, cook through
  3. Sauce: Mix listed ingredients — all use 150g 0% Greek yogurt base
